# InspectorHub

**Inspection software built with home and commercial inspectors, and open for anyone to look at.**

We make the tools an inspector uses from the first booking to the signed report: the booking page, the inspection itself on a phone or tablet, the report your client reads, and the agreement they sign. The software is open source, so you can see how it works, and you can run your own copy if you ever want to.

## What we are building

- **Inspection work that goes with you.** A fast editor for ratings, comments and photos, built for a crawlspace with one bar of signal. Your notes are kept on the device and sync when you are back online.
- **Reports clients and agents can use.** Clear, branded reports, agreements signed on any device with a record that can be checked later, and simple ways to share a report with the people who need it.
- **Booking that does not take your evenings.** A booking page for your clients, scheduling for your team, and calendar sync so you are not booked twice.
- **Software you can inspect.** Open source from the start, so you can see exactly how it works and run your own copy.

## Where things stand

- The web app is available now at [inspectorhub.io](https://inspectorhub.io).
- The Android app is available from its [releases page](https://github.com/InspectorHub/OpenInspection-App/releases). The iPhone, iPad and Windows apps are in development and not yet available.
- We do not keep a feature list here, because it would go out of date. What changed and when is in the [release notes](https://github.com/InspectorHub/OpenInspection/releases) and the [update log](https://inspectorhub.io/changelog). If something is not listed there, assume it is not ready yet.

## Our goals

1. Make the work in the field faster, not just the paperwork afterwards.
2. Be plain about what works today and what is still being built.
3. Treat your records with care, and say plainly what you can take with you.
4. Build it in the open and listen to the people who use it.

## Take a look

- **Try it:** [inspectorhub.io](https://inspectorhub.io) has a hosted version you can start without installing anything.
